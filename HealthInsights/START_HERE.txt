🚀 WHAT THIS APP DOES:

   • Automatically syncs with Apple Health (no manual imports!)
   • Shows real-time health stats (steps, heart rate, sleep, calories)
   • Generates AI-powered health insights
   • Creates beautiful trend charts
   • Provides daily and on-demand analysis
   • 100% private - all data stays on your device

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💡 REQUIREMENTS:

   ✓ Xcode 14.0+
   ✓ iPhone with iOS 16.0+
   ✓ Apple Developer account (free or paid)
   ✓ Physical iPhone (HealthKit doesn't work on Simulator)
